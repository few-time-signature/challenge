# Provenance

This organizer submission uses the fixed `grs-cc-v1` graph and Boolean encoder and the public GRS-CC reduction in `formal/PoissonDAG/Constructions/GRSCC/Certification.lean` at challenge revision `6e53dcf0999fbfb975974af4695d88b1ab78df48`. The protocol history is linked on the challenge website. [Design reference](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2502)

The submitted Lean modules formalize the bounded digit traversal, its equivalence to the organizer's mask counts, and extraction of the twenty moments from 63 polynomial evaluations. Each witness is reproduced from the definitions in the proof. No new axioms are assumed.
